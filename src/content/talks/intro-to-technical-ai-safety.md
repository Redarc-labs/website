An introduction to technical AI safety for a technical audience that is new to it. The talk starts with the vocabulary, because much of the confusion in the field comes from the same word meaning different things to different people: what alignment means and what it does not, what an evaluation actually measures, what a model organism is, and why interpretability and control are different bets on the same problem. It then walks through the main subfields one at a time: alignment and post-training, evaluations and dangerous-capability testing, AI control and monitoring, mechanistic interpretability, and biosecurity screening for biological models. For each one: the open problems, which of them can be worked on today with open models and a laptop, and where the people doing that work publish and meet.
